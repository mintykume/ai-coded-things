# Vinyl Label Studio

Put any picture on a vinyl record, frame it, and save the record drop as a video. The record lands at 2.3 seconds and the needle drops at 4.3 seconds, so you know exactly where to start your music in the edit.

**Why?** For video edits: a record intro with your own picture on the label, made without editing software.

**How:**
1. Open `index.html` in Chrome or Safari on a computer.
2. Drop a picture onto the record, then frame it with the sliders.
3. Choose the format (9:16, 16:9 or square) and the background (including green screen).
4. Click record. The video downloads when the pass finishes. It's MP4 where the browser supports it, otherwise WebM, which CapCut and DaVinci Resolve open.

Made with AI. One HTML file. The picture never leaves your computer.
