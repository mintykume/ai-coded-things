# Thirty Sits

A 30-day meditation challenge tracker. For each day you log that you sat, for how long, what kind of sit it was, how calm you felt before and after, and one thing you noticed. It shows your streak, your total minutes and the average "calm lift", and has a short weekly reflection.

**Why?** To make a habit visible without making it a chore: logging a day takes about 30 seconds.

**How:** open `index.html` and set the day you started. Entries are saved in your browser on this device only; nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data clears them.

Made with AI. One HTML file.
